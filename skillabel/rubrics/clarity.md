# Rubric: clarity

Grades how legibly the skill states its steps, inputs, and outputs — the
contract a model (and a human reviewer) must execute. Description economy
matters: the always-in-context description should be tight; the body should be
scannable. Ground every finding in a cited line.

**A** — Numbered, imperative steps; inputs and outputs named explicitly; tools
per step identified. A reader can restate what the skill needs, does, and
produces without re-reading. No filler prose or redundant restatement.

**C** — The intent is understandable but structure is loose: prose paragraphs
instead of steps, or inputs/outputs implied rather than named. A reader can
follow it but must infer some of the contract.

**F** — Rambling or contradictory; steps missing or out of order; inputs and
outputs unstated. A reader cannot reliably tell what the skill will do. The
description pads context without adding predictive value.
